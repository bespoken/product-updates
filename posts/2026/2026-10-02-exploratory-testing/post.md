Before you can test an IVR, you need to know what it does. That's often the hard part. Menus go undocumented, change without warning, or were built by another team or a vendor. So mapping one usually means calling it again and again with a notepad.

**Exploratory Testing turns all of that into one live session.** Call the number from your browser, move through the menus, and Bespoken draws the map as you go.

## Explore live, right from your browser

Enter a phone number and press **Call**. Use the dialpad to press keys, or hold to talk when the IVR expects a spoken answer. You'll see a live transcript and a call timer the whole time. If the line goes quiet for too long, Bespoken hangs up for you, so a call never stays open by accident.

## Watch your call tree build itself

Every prompt you hear is added to an interactive map, and every choice you make adds a new branch. You can read the map left to right or top to bottom. When the same prompt plays more than once, it appears once on the map with a count, so the map stays easy to read.

## Know exactly where to go next

The map shows how many options you haven't followed yet. You can see which parts of the IVR you've covered and which still need a call.

## Inspect and refine any step

Click any step on the map to play its recording, correct the transcript, or add and edit the keypad and spoken options for that menu. Your map stays accurate even when the audio isn't perfect.

## From exploration to regression tests in one click

When the IVR is mapped, **generate a ready-to-run test suite** from it. Exploration becomes regression coverage without writing tests by hand. You can also export the full prompt list, and a diagram for Lucid to share with your team.

## Get started

Open **Exploratory Tests** in the Bespoken Dashboard and click **New exploratory test**. Give it a name, enter a phone number, and start calling. Bespoken maps the menus as you go.
