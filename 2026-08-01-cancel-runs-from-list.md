---
title: Cancel a running test from the suite list
description: Owners and admins can now cancel an in-flight run without opening the suite.
tags: New, Feature
---

Hovering the active-run badge on the test suite list now offers a **Cancel**
action for organization owners and admins.

Cancellation is confirmed server-side and can take up to ~15 seconds to take
effect; the badge shows a "Cancelling…" state in the meantime.
